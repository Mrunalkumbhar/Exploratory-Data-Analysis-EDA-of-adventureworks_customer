# Education Level Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand the educational background of customers and determine how education influences income and customer distribution.

---

## Business Questions

### Q18. How many customers belong to each education level?

| Education Level     | Customers |
| ------------------- | --------: |
| Bachelors           |     5,261 |
| Partial College     |     4,966 |
| High School         |     3,241 |
| Graduate Degree     |     3,125 |
| Partial High School |     1,555 |

---

### Q19. Which education level is the most common?

**Answer:** **Bachelors** is the most common education level with **5,261 customers**.

---

### Q20. What is the average annual income by education level?

| Education Level     | Average Annual Income |
| ------------------- | --------------------: |
| Graduate Degree     |            $66,150.40 |
| Bachelors           |            $64,396.50 |
| Partial College     |            $55,090.62 |
| High School         |            $48,963.28 |
| Partial High School |            $39,575.56 |

---

### Q21. What is the gender distribution by education level?

| Education Level     |  Male | Female |
| ------------------- | ----: | -----: |
| Bachelors           | 2,685 |  2,576 |
| Partial College     | 2,496 |  2,470 |
| High School         | 1,686 |  1,555 |
| Graduate Degree     | 1,568 |  1,557 |
| Partial High School |   800 |    755 |

---

# Key Insights

* Bachelor's degree holders represent the largest customer segment.
* Graduate Degree customers have the highest average annual income.
* Customers with higher education levels generally earn higher average annual incomes.
* Male and female customers are nearly equally distributed across every education category.
* The dataset indicates a positive relationship between education level and income.
